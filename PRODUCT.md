# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mixed primary audience: students, adult learners, and other visitors discovering an Arabic learning platform. First action on the marketing home is not locked to one persona — the surface must welcome a mixed crowd and lead them toward exploring courses or creating an account.

## Product Purpose

يَطْمَئِن is an Arabic (RTL) learning platform for published courses, lessons, quizzes, and progress tracking. Learners request enrollment; the team reviews requests before access. Success means a visitor understands the calm, ordered learning path and takes a clear next step (browse courses or register).

## Positioning

An Arabic learning path built around reassurance (طمأنينة): ordered lessons, human-reviewed enrollment, and follow-up — not a noisy marketplace or self-serve binge. The home page should feel special and distinct from generic edtech SaaS; visual distinctiveness is explicitly in scope.

## Operating Context

Public marketing site (home, catalog, instructors, how-it-works, about, FAQ, contact) plus authenticated role dashboards (student, instructor, admin, and supporting roles). Live production reference: https://yatmaen.ammarelgndy.cloud/

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Arabic UI only; RTL throughout; no language switcher.
- Stack: Laravel 12, Blade, Tailwind CSS v4, Alpine.js, Vite.
- Enrollment is request-and-approve, not instant open access.
- Open: which proof assets (photos, course covers) will be real vs authored placeholders for the redesign.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: يَطْمَئِن (and Latin slug/domain forms such as yatmaen).
- Full visual replacement of the public home (and its visual world) is approved: new palette, type, and composition allowed. Product truth and Arabic RTL remain fixed.
- Prior teal/indigo/amber SaaS look is not binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live site and seeded demo courses/instructors (synthetic demo content).
- No confirmed testimonials, press, or photography library yet — do not invent commercial claims; illustrative/synthetic imagery must be labeled when it could be mistaken for real.

## Product Principles

1. Reassure before hype — the name’s promise (calm progress) is the product’s emotional contract.
2. Make the next step obvious for a mixed audience without persona clutter.
3. Stay Arabic-first and RTL-native in every surface decision.
4. Prefer distinct craft over category-default edtech templates.
5. Never fabricate social proof, pricing, or capability the product does not have.
